Content Generation

Build a structured prompt for CRISC instructional content: lesson text, scenario questions in the CRISC style, a one-page domain summary, flashcards, worked calculations, or an assignment with a rubric. Set the part, topic, course context, and rigor, and the prompt assembles as you go.
