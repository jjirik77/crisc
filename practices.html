CRISC Practices

Nine self-checking activities that turn each lesson into the judgment the exam rewards. Sort, rank, calculate, and work through exam-style cases as a risk practitioner, watch your self-check meter build, and finish with a practice record you can print or save as a PDF. Practice here, then use any activity as a model for your own courses.

Activities: 9Each: 35 to 40 minDomains: All fourFormat: Self-checkingPractice record: Print or PDF

How practice works

Concept practice for every lesson

Stations

Each activity has four or five practice stations and a reflection: sorts, rankings, calculations, and exam-style cases.

Self-checking

Every answer is checked on demand with the risk practitioner’s reasoning. Retry any station as often as needed.

Self-check meter

A meter shows how each concept is going as you practice. It is a self-check, not a score, and the practice record suggests what to review next.

Practice record

Print or save a record of the stations you completed, your answers, items to revisit, and your reflections. It can serve as documentation for continuing education (CPE).

For faculty

Using the practices in class

  • Practice first: work each activity as a learner would. The goal is practice and “muscle memory” for the exam’s judgment calls, not a grade. Nothing here is scored.
  • Use it as a model: after you finish, look at how the activity is built: the station types, the item-level feedback, and the reflection. Reuse that pattern in your own courses.
  • Practice record: print the practice record or save it as a PDF. It lists the stations you completed, your answers with the practitioner’s reasoning, items to revisit, your reflections, and time spent. Keep it as CPE documentation if you need it.
  • Placement: assign each activity after the lesson reading it reinforces. Each card links to that lesson section.
  • In class: run a station as a think-pair-share, then let pairs check answers and compare the practitioner’s reasoning.
  • Team mode: a pair or team can work one activity together and list every member on the practice record.
