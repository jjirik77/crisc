Advise the owner; never take the owner’s decision. CRISC rewards the answer a risk practitioner would give: start from business objectives, identify and assess before you respond, make sure every risk and control has an accountable owner, and report in terms the business can act on. Every lesson trains that judgment alongside the content.

Exam Blueprint

Domain Weights at a Glance

The exam draws 150 questions from four domains. Risk Response and Reporting carries almost a third of the exam, so three lessons cover it. Governance comes next, and it frames every other domain.

D1: Governance26%
Lessons 1 and 2~39 questions
D2: Risk Assessment22%
Lessons 3 and 4~33 questions
D3: Risk Response and Reporting32%
Lessons 5, 6, and 7~48 questions
D4: Technology and Security20%
Lessons 8 and 9~30 questions

What changed in the current outline. Two domains were renamed: IT Risk Assessment became Risk Assessment, and Information Technology and Security became Technology and Security. Two points of weight moved from Domain 4 (22% to 20%) to Domain 2 (20% to 22%). The outline also adds explicit topics such as threat modeling, exceptions and exemptions, emerging-risk reporting, and facilitating tabletop exercises, and ISACA points to AI risk throughout. Older study guides may show the previous weights.

About This Workshop

Built for Faculty and Program Leads

Who this is for

This workshop is for faculty and program leads who are preparing for the CRISC exam and who want to bring IT risk management into their courses. It assumes working knowledge of security fundamentals and focuses on the judgment the exam rewards.

Each lesson pairs exam preparation with a faculty teaching segment: a signature assignment, rubric criteria, a lab idea, and where the topic fits in a program. eMates in every lesson give you short interactive refreshers, and many of them are ready to use with students.

How to use this resource

Work through the lessons in order. Lesson 0 explains the exam and the mindset. Lessons 1 to 8 follow the outline part by part. Lesson 9 finishes Domain 4 and brings everything together with a capstone, exam strategy, and a curriculum map. This site complements the official ISACA review materials and does not reproduce them.
